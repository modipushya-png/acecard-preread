What AceCard actually does (D2C side)

A baseline so the industry mapping below makes sense. Skip if you know it.

The eight things in one product

  • NFC card. Black-and-gold, premium build, ₹3,000 anchor, no app install on prospect's side.
  • Fully customisable profile. Video backgrounds, custom colours, fonts, galleries, products, articles, embedded files, configurable lead form. No competitor in India offers this depth at a one-time price.
  • Zero AceCard branding by default. The page that opens looks like the user's brand or company. TapOnn, Popl, V1CE all charge to remove their watermark. AceCard ships clean.
  • Meta + Google + LinkedIn remarketing pixels. Fire on every tap. No NFC card competitor has this at any price tier. Every handshake becomes a retargeting audience.
  • Voice capture + AI structuring. 90 seconds of speech after a meeting becomes structured CRM data: contact, company, deal size, objections, next steps.
  • 4 CRM integrations live. HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Odoo. Pipedrive on roadmap.
  • Trackable links. See which prospects opened which collateral.
  • Bulk admin dashboard. Pre-configure 200+ cards with brand consistency, then let individuals personalise within the brand frame.

Three filters used to rank industries below: (1) Do they exchange physical cards in person? (2) Does follow-up after the meeting drive revenue? (3) Does the person carrying the card care about how their digital identity looks? The more "yes" the higher the tier.

01 · Real Estate

Site sales executives · brokers · channel partners · luxury developers
Reality
A site sales exec at a Lodha or DLF launch handles 50–150 walk-ins on a weekend. A broker does 5–10 site visits a day across multiple builders. An average deal cycle is 4 to 7 in-person touches before a token cheque.
What's broken
"We have a builder CRM. We have a Channel Partner WhatsApp group. We have walk-in registers. None of them talk to each other. By Monday morning, half the weekend's walk-ins don't exist anywhere." Walk-in data captured on paper or a Google form rarely reaches the developer's marketing team. Meta retargeting on walk-ins is impossible because pixels never fired.
AceCard fit
Sales exec taps card on prospect → fully branded developer profile opens (project gallery, drone walkthrough, floorplans, virtual tour, EMI calculator, channel partner agreement form). Pixel fires — that prospect is now in Meta and Google retargeting audiences for that specific project. Voice capture after site visit logs budget, BHK preference, possession timeline, family stage. Pushes to the developer's Salesforce or Zoho.
Bonus
For brokers, the "your brand, not ours" feature is critical. A broker is selling themselves, not us. They get an Instagram-grade portfolio page that costs nothing per month.

08 · Pharma / Medical Representatives

Medical reps (MRs) · area business managers · KAMs · CRO/CDMO sales
Reality
An MR at Sun Pharma or Cipla makes 8–15 doctor visits per day. Each call is 5–7 minutes — pitch a molecule, leave samples, log it. Compliance is brutal: UCPMP code, no gifts, sample tracking, prescription audit. India has ~1 million MRs across pharma + medical devices.
What's broken
Existing pharma SFA tools (Veeva, PharmaACE, Trillium) are heavy desktop apps. MRs scribble notes on a printed Daily Call Report. Doctor's prescription patterns aren't tied back to specific MR visits. High MR turnover. Field activity verification is the manager's nightmare.
AceCard fit
MR's profile = brand product list with clinical evidence, e-detailing slides, sample request form, e-prescription pad. Tap during clinic visit = location-stamped, time-stamped — manager sees activity without GPS-stalking. Voice notes after the call log: doctor's main objection, sample SKUs left, peer-product mentioned, prescribing rate. Syncs to Veeva or pharma in-house SFA.
Caveat
Compliance: pharma marketing is governed by UCPMP/EFPIA codes. The remarketing pixel needs careful handling — to doctors directly retargeting branded medication ads is legally restricted in India and the EU. Position pixels for medical device, hospital diagnostic, OTC, nutraceutical sub-segments instead.
