Accelerate — $625M savings

At 500 reps in India alone: ~1,000 hours of admin saved daily. Multiply by loaded cost. The number is not small.

Section 06 · The Proposal
The proposal

A 90-day pilot.
No integration risk. Three measurable outcomes.

AceCard sits above your existing SAP/Salesforce stack. No migration. No replacement. A layer that feeds data in — better, faster, more complete than before.

Recommended pilot
USL India — Key On-Trade Territory. 90 Days.

50 field reps. Key metro markets — Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru. On-trade accounts (bars, restaurants, hotels, clubs). AceCard deployed alongside existing CRM. Three baseline metrics captured at Day 0, measured at Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90.

Week 1: Deploy AceCards to 50 reps. 30-minute onboarding. No app training. Just tap and talk.
Weeks 2–12: Reps use AceCard on normal field visits. CRM data is compared week-on-week.
Day 90: Three-metric readout. If the numbers hold, discuss scale rollout across USL.
Metric 1
CRM Fill Rate

% of field visits that generate a CRM entry within 24 hours. Baseline measured at Day 0 (industry benchmark: ~30%). Target at Day 90: 85%+.

Metric 2
Rep Time-on-Admin

Minutes per week spent on CRM logging, measured via rep self-report and system timestamps. Baseline: ~2 hours/week. Target: under 30 minutes/week.

Metric 3
Pipeline Data Completeness

Average number of structured fields populated per CRM entry. Baseline: 2–3 fields. With AceCard: 6–8 fields (contact, account, interest, size, timeline, next step).
